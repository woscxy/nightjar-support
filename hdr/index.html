Nightjar HDR test

This page has an HDR photo and an HDR video. In each, the left square is as bright as HDR goes and the right one is ordinary white.

You need a screen that can show HDR (iPhone 12 or later, a recent iPad Pro, or a Mac with an XDR or Liquid Retina display), iOS 26, iPadOS 26 or Safari 26 or later, and the screen brightness below maximum.

  • With Nightjar on and Limit HDR brightness on (the default), the left squares look plain white, like any other white on the screen.
  • In Nightjar's Settings, switch Limit HDR brightness off: the left squares glow brighter than everything else on the screen.
